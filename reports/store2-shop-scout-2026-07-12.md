## Why these areas work for BnB

- **Chinar Park/Atghara (pick #2):** the food hub at New Town's edge. Heavy cafe/restro cluster means people already come there to eat and snack. Residential towers all around, airport road traffic, 5 min from New Town core.
- **Kestopur-VIP Road belt (picks #1, 3-6):** cheapest frontage in the zone, dense residential, strong evening market footfall, and the metro corridor improves it further. A ₹19-22K main-road shop here leaves budget headroom for fit-out.
- **New Town core:** best brand visibility (CC2, Axis Mall, Eco Space crowd) but nothing retail at this budget right now. The scout watches it daily.

## Caveats before you sign anything

1. **Verify on the ground.** OLX listings are broker-heavy; expect 1 month brokerage and 3-6 months deposit (rarely stated in the ad).
2. **"Office" units** (picks #7, #8, value flag) may not permit F&B use. Ask about trade licence, water connection and ventilation before visiting.
3. Listing #3 and #1 may be the same broker recycling stock; both are "main road Kestopur". One phone call covers both.
4. Prices ending in 001 (₹19,001, ₹22,001) are broker-style placeholder prices; expect mild negotiation room.
5. Pick #6 asks ₹1 lakh advance (four months of rent up front). Pick #7's search-page numbers were stale; the item page says ₹25,000 and 200 sqft carpet, corrected above.
