## 2. Step-by-step in Petpooja

> Don't touch/delete the old "- 25" categories until the new menu is verified live, or Zomato shows nothing in the gap.

**Step 1 — Open the Zomato channel**
Menu → Menu & Discounts → Manage Menu → **Zomato** tile.

**Step 2 — Add the 39 items**
For each new category (Soda, Coffee, Fruit Tea, Milk Tea, Matcha, Smoothie, Chocolate): click the category → **Add Items** → tick the items from the table above → Save. (These categories are currently empty in the Zomato channel.)

**Step 3 — Cold-only + price, per item (the confirmed method)**
Working **inside the Zomato channel menu** (this keeps Hot alive for dine-in):
- Expose **only the Cold [350ML]** variation; do not add/expose Hot.
- Set the Zomato price from the table above.
- Tick **"Online Orders" / "O"** (expose in online order) — it's off on the new items.
- Available toggle ON.

**Step 4 — Upload images**
Menu → **Multi-Item Images Upload** → upload all 39 from the `New_menu_without_sparkles` folder (filenames are now cleaned, they match item names exactly). Note: these are AI/studio images; if Zomato's review flags any, swap in a real photo for that item.

**Step 5 — Push**
In the Zomato menu, click **Menu Trigger**. Wait for "Last Menu Triggered" to update to today with **Status: Success**.

**Step 6 — Verify live**
Open zomato.com → Blend N Bubbles, Barrackpore. Check categories, the 39 prices, images, and no leftover duplicates.

**Step 7 — Remove the old menu (only after Step 6 passes)**
Empty/delete the old "- 25" categories (Milk Tea - 25, Fruit Tea Fusions - 25, Smoothies - 25, Coffee - 25). Trigger again, verify.
